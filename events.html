D.R.A.F.T.S. Mentorship Program

Driven Representatives Aspiring For Transcendence and Stewardship — our ongoing mentorship program, not a one-off event. It runs continuously, pairing members with guidance for personal and professional growth throughout their time in JPCS.

Members are paired with officers and senior peers who've walked the same path, for one-on-one guidance whenever it's needed.
