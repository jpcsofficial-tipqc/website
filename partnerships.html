JPCS – T.I.P. QC Chapter grows through the organizations, chapters, and communities we build alongside. We're proud of every partnership on this page — and always looking for the next one.

OUR MOTHER ORGANIZATION

Philippine Computer Society — National Chapter

PCS

JPCS – T.I.P. QC Chapter operates under the Philippine Computer Society (PCS) National Chapter as its mother organization — the longest-existing professional association of computing and information technology in the Philippines.

That affiliation, renewed every year, is what connects every member of our chapter to a nationwide professional IT community — long after graduation, not just for four years on campus.
